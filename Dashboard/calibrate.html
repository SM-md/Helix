Procedure
1

Open this page on a device connected to the Pi's Wi-Fi.

2

Click "Start Capture" then slowly rotate the rover one full 360° in open air, away from motors and large metal. Takes ~45 s.

3

Watch the ellipse close into a circle on the canvas. Stop when quality says Good or you hit 200 samples.

4

Set declination & mount offset, then go to Code Output and paste the patch into app.py.

How to apply
1

Copy the compass_loop patch. In app.py, find the heading calculation block inside compass_loop() and replace with the patch.

2

Copy the lidar_loop patch. In app.py, find the sector extraction lines inside lidar_loop() and replace with the patch.

3

Restart the app on the Pi:
sudo systemctl restart helix   or   python3 app.py

4

Back on the dashboard, confirm the heading arrow points north when the rover faces north, and the radar reacts only to real obstacles in front.
